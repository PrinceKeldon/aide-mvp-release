# Building AIDE In Public

Subtitle: A public series on building a personal agent operating layer

Last updated: April 6, 2026

## Purpose

This page is the landing point for the public AIDE writing series.

It is meant to do three things:

- explain the series in one place
- show the current reading order
- connect the long-form essays to the shorter X threads and build logs

## Series Thesis

AIDE is being built as a personal operating layer:

- one agent
- one memory
- many trusted devices
- one approval and action fabric
- one daily operating surface across inbox, calendar, and execution

And beyond that:

- sovereign agent-to-agent collaboration through M-Peer

In plain language:

**My AIDE can work across my devices, help run my day, and eventually collaborate with your AIDE without either of us giving up control.**

## Who This Series Is For

This series is for:

- builders interested in agents, memory, trust, and workflow systems
- product thinkers exploring what comes after chat-first AI
- non-technical readers who want to understand what a useful personal AI system could actually look like
